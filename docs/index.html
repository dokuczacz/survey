Submit an indicative commitment to the 2026 Antalya Pledges on AI

Building on the COP29 Declaration on Green Digital Action, a High-Level Green Digital Action Roundtable on Artificial Intelligence will be held on 16 November 2026 during COP31 in Antalya.

Speaking opportunities will be prioritized for governments, organizations and stakeholders prepared to announce, support or advance concrete commitments for the environmentally sustainable, transparent and inclusive development and use of AI through measurable, voluntary action.

Please use this form to provide an initial indication of your proposed commitment.
